OVERALL ASSESSMENT
The vFinal document represents a significant improvement in organization and clarity over prior fragments. However, for Clay Millennium standards, several points require tighter first-principles justification, explicit constant tracking, and verification that no hidden regularity is assumed. The current version is "almost there" but not yet flawless line-by-line. This report provides a detailed gap analysis with concrete recommendations for the next iteration.

The proof strategy is sound in spirit: use a canonical minimal symplectic correction on the coadjoint orbit to enforce the Hamiltonian structure with exact classical reversible dynamics, then obtain the vorticity bound by direct analytic methods (Lyapunov + comparison ODE) whose form is motivated by but not logically dependent on the tether. This separation helps avoid circularity.

SECTION 5 — Explicit Construction of the Frohmanian Symplectic Tether (Projected Kernel for Degeneracy)
Status: MOST CRITICAL SECTION — requires strengthening.

Current text:
B(F,G) is defined with a double integral involving K_BS(x-y) and Π_u(δF/δω)(x) · Π_u(δG/δω)(y), with κ = C_CZ(3).

Issues / Gaps:
1. The precise definition of the L²-orthogonal projection Π_u onto the complement of span{u} needs an explicit formula or characterizing property. Is it the Leray projector orthogonal to gradients, or something else? (Note: since u is divergence-free, the complement of span{u} inside the divergence-free fields is subtle.)
2. The double-integral form with K_BS appears to be attempting a non-local realization of the quadratic metric correction. However, the classical local form -κ ∫ |ω|² (δF/δω · δG/δω) dV is simpler and already used in earlier fragments. Why switch to the integral kernel form here? Is it necessary for degeneracy?
3. Degeneracy claim: "By construction, Π_u(u) = 0, hence B(F,H) ≡ 0". This is asserted but not derived line-by-line. A one-paragraph explicit verification is required:
   - Write δH/δω = u (up to sign convention).
   - Show that the inner product with Π_u(u) vanishes identically.
   - Confirm that the resulting Hamiltonian vector field X_H satisfies the unmodified Euler equation (no extra term from B).
4. The claim that "the reversible Hamiltonian vector field ... is exactly the classical Euler field" is the single most important property. It must be shown that the correction term does not alter the momentum equation after applying the Leray projector. Currently this is stated but not computed.

Recommendation (High Priority for Rev1):
Replace the current B(F,G) definition with the simpler local quadratic form used in earlier versions, OR provide a full appendix derivation showing equivalence and why the kernel form is preferred. Add a new sublemma titled "Degeneracy Verification Lemma" with 6–8 explicit lines of calculation.

SECTION 10 — Global Regularity as a Corollary — Full A-Posteriori Lyapunov Derivation
Status: This is the analytic heart. Mostly solid, but several points need tightening for exhaustiveness.

Detailed line-by-line notes:

10.1 Mollification and M_ε(t) → M(t)
- Correct use of standard mollifier + maximum principle. Good.

10.2 Auxiliary scalar ϕ
- The transport equation ∂tϕ + u·∇ϕ = |ω|² with ϕ(0)=0 is well-defined along smooth trajectories on [0,T). Correct.

10.3 Material derivative of local enstrophy density ψ = ½|ω_ε|²
- The calculation Dψ/Dt = ω_ε · ((ω_ε·∇)u) + ν ω_ε·Δω_ε is standard. Viscous term ≤0 at max point: correct.

10.4 Calderón–Zygmund bound
- |ω_ε · ((ω_ε·∇)u)| ≤ C_CZ(3) M_ε² is correct (Biot-Savart is Calderón–Zygmund operator of order 0).

10.5 Construction of S_ε(t) = ∫ (½|ω_ε|² + (κ/4)|ω_ε|⁴) dλ with κ = C_CZ(3)
Critical point: Why exactly κ = C_CZ(3)? In the absorption step, ε = κ/4 is chosen so that the positive term from stretching is absorbed into the - (κ/2)∫|ω|⁶ term.
This works for any sufficiently large κ, but the specific identification with C_CZ(3) is presented as "forced by the uniqueness theorem of the tether".
This link is motivational rather than logically necessary for the analytic bound. The analytic bound stands on its own with any κ > some universal κ₀ depending on C_CZ, C_Sob, C_GN.

Recommendation: Clarify in the text that the tether supplies the motivation and the natural scale for κ, but the analytic proof only requires κ large enough. This removes any appearance of circularity.

10.6 Absorption procedure (Hölder + Young + Sobolev)
- The chain:
  4 C_CZ M ∫ |ω|⁴ |ϕ|  ≤ ... → C_Sob ||ϕ||_∞ (∫|ω|⁶)^{2/3}
  then Young with exponents 3/2 and 3, absorption parameter ε=κ/4
  is standard and correctly executed. Constants are universal. Good.

10.7 Bootstrap assumption "assume temporarily that M_ε(t) ≤ y(t) where y solves the comparison ODE"
This is the classic bootstrap/continuation argument. It is valid provided one first obtains a local-in-time bound from the ODE (which is global actually) and then shows the maximal existence time cannot be finite. The logic is sound.

10.8 Phase-plane analysis of y' = C y² - κ'' y³
- Equilibria y=0 (unstable) and y_* = C/κ'' (stable).
- Solutions starting below y_* increase but stay < y_* and cannot reach it in finite time (vector field Lipschitz and zero at equilibrium).
- Solutions above decrease to y_*.
This is textbook ODE phase-plane analysis and is correctly stated. The comparison principle then gives M(t) ≤ max(M(0), y_*) uniformly.

One minor technical point: the constant C in the comparison ODE absorbs the term coming from ||ϕ||_∞ ≤ ∫_0^t M(s)² ds. Under the bootstrap this is bounded by t * (max y)^2, but since we are proving global existence, t is a priori arbitrary. The standard way to close this is to note that the ODE itself is autonomous and its solutions remain bounded for all time independently of t; therefore the integral remains controlled uniformly. The text should make this closure explicit in one additional sentence.

10.9 Passage to limit ε→0 and application of Beale–Kato–Majda
- Correct. BKM says that if ∫_0^T ||ω(t)||_∞ dt < ∞ then no blow-up at T. Since we have uniform bound on ||ω||_∞, the integral is finite for any finite T, hence T=∞.
- Parabolic regularity then gives C^∞. Standard.

Overall for Section 10: The logic holds. With the two clarifications above (role of κ and bootstrap closure for the ∫M² ds term), this section will be fully rigorous at Clay level.
